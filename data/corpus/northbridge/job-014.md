# Job: Data Quality Specialist at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Data Quality Specialist to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will coach business teams in using data in their decisions; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation.

Salary: AUD 110,000 to AUD 125,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
