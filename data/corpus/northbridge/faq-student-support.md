# Student support FAQ

**Can I get an extension?**
Your course coordinator can approve an extension of up to five days.

**How do I appeal a grade?**
Lodge an appeal through the student portal within 14 days of the result being released.

**Who can I talk to about study problems?**
Contact our Student Services Advisor, Chloe Wiremu.
