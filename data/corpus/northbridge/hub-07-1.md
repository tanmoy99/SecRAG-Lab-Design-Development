# Access control: common mistakes to avoid

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Access reviews are often skipped because managers find them tedious and unclear.

Shared accounts make it impossible to tell who did what, so they should be phased out. Least privilege means each person can reach only the data they need for their role.

Small organisations can get most of the benefit of access control with a few well-designed groups. Removing access promptly when staff change roles is as important as granting it correctly.

This topic is covered in more depth in SC310 Security for Data Professionals.
