# Dashboard design: a checklist for small teams

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. Ask users what decisions the dashboard supports before choosing any charts.

Too many charts on one screen make it harder, not easier, to see what matters. Consistent colours and clear labels matter more than visual effects.

Every dashboard should say where its data comes from and when it was last refreshed. A good dashboard answers a small number of questions for a specific audience.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Data Visualisation and Storytelling, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
