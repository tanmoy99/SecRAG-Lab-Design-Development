# Assessment Extension Policy

Students may request more time to complete an assessment task if illness, caring responsibilities, work demands or other circumstances beyond their control affect their study.

The maximum extension a course coordinator can approve is five days.

Requests should be made before the due date through the student portal, with supporting documents where available.
