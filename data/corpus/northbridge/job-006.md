# Job: Senior Data Analyst at Ironbark Insurance

Ironbark Insurance is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders.

Salary: AUD 120,000 to AUD 135,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@ironbark.example.
