# Job: Senior Data Analyst at Southern Water Authority

Southern Water Authority is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; support the response to information security incidents.

Salary: AUD 105,000 to AUD 120,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@southernwater.example.
