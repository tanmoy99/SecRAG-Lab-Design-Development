# Continuing Professional Development Policy

## Purpose

Continuing professional development (CPD) keeps members' knowledge current. This policy sets out how much CPD each membership tier must complete and how CPD records are checked.

## Annual requirement

- Associate: 10 hours per membership year
- Member: twenty hours per membership year
- Fellow: 25 hours per membership year

Student members have no minimum CPD requirement. Hours may come from courses, conferences, structured reading, mentoring or presenting at professional events.

## Audit

Each year the Institute audits a random sample of 10% of members with a CPD requirement. Selected members must provide evidence of their recorded activities.

Members who do not meet the requirement are contacted by the Membership team and given a chance to make up the shortfall before their membership lapses.
