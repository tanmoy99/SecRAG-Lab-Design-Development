# Job: Business Intelligence Developer at Coastal Health Network

Coastal Health Network is looking for a Business Intelligence Developer to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will support the response to information security incidents; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform.

Salary: AUD 105,000 to AUD 120,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@coastalhealth.example.
