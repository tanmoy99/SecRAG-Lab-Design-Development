# Records disposal: a checklist for small teams

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Legal holds override normal disposal schedules when litigation or an investigation is likely.

Records should only be destroyed under an approved retention and disposal authority. Digital records are often kept forever by default because no one is responsible for deleting them.

A disposal register shows what was destroyed, when, and under which authority. Disposing of records on schedule reduces storage costs and the impact of any data breach.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Records and Information Management, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
