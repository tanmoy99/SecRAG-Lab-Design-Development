# Data Quality Management (DQ150)

Learn how to define, measure and improve the quality of the data your organisation relies on. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Data quality dimensions
- Profiling and validation rules
- Root-cause analysis of data defects
- Data quality scorecards

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: A$1,725 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

The next intake begins on 22/03/2027 and runs for sixteen weeks. Delivery is blended: online study plus two full-day workshops at the City campus.

## Course coordinator

Your course coordinator is Yuki Murray (yuki.murray@northbridge.example), who can help with questions about the content, assessment or study load.

## Assessment

Assessment is based on two written assignments and a final project that applies the course content to a real or realistic workplace problem. There is no closed-book exam.

Applications open about eight weeks before each intake. Once your enrolment is confirmed you will receive access to the learning portal. If you need to withdraw, see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
