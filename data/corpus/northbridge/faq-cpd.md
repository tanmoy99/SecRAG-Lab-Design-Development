# CPD FAQ

**How many CPD hours do I need?**
Associate members need 10 hours. Member members need 20 hours. Fellow members need twenty-five hours.

**Will my CPD be audited?**
Possibly. Each year ten per cent of members are randomly selected.

**What counts as CPD?**
Courses, conferences, structured reading, mentoring and presenting all count.
