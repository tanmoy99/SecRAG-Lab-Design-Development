# Data literacy: a practical introduction

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Measuring data literacy with a short self-assessment gives a baseline to improve against.

Short, practical sessions built around staff's own reports work better than generic training. Data literacy is the ability to read, question and communicate with data in everyday work.

Most organisations overestimate how comfortable their staff are with basic charts and percentages. Managers set the tone: if they ask where a number came from, their teams start asking too.
