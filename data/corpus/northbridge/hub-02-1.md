# Data literacy: common mistakes to avoid

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Data literacy is the ability to read, question and communicate with data in everyday work.

Managers set the tone: if they ask where a number came from, their teams start asking too. Most organisations overestimate how comfortable their staff are with basic charts and percentages.

Measuring data literacy with a short self-assessment gives a baseline to improve against. Short, practical sessions built around staff's own reports work better than generic training.
