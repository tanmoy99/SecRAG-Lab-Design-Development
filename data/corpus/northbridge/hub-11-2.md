# Business glossaries: common mistakes to avoid

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. Link glossary terms to the reports and data sets that use them so people can find the definition in context.

Arguments about numbers in meetings are often really arguments about definitions. Each glossary term needs an owner who can approve changes to its definition.

A glossary that is not maintained quickly loses trust and stops being used. A business glossary defines the terms an organisation uses, such as 'active member' or 'enrolment'.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Metadata and Data Catalogues, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
