# Privacy by design: a checklist for small teams

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. A privacy impact assessment early in a project is far cheaper than redesigning a finished system.

Default settings matter: most users never change them, so the default should be the private option. Privacy by design means building privacy protections into a system from the start rather than adding them later.

Collecting less personal information is the simplest and most effective privacy control. Retention periods should be decided when data is collected, not when storage runs out.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Privacy Law for Practitioners, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
