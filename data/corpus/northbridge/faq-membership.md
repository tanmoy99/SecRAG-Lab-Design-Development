# Membership FAQ

**How much does membership cost?**
Student: AUD 90; Associate: $185.00; Member: $415.00; Fellow: $635.00 per year.

**Can I upgrade my membership?**
Yes. Upgrades are charged at the difference between the two annual fees.

**How do I become a Fellow?**
Fellowship is by invitation. Members can be nominated by two existing Fellows.
