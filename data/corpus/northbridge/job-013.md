# Job: Data Steward at Tasman University

Tasman University is looking for a Data Steward to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; support the response to information security incidents; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects.

Salary: AUD 130,000 to AUD 145,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@tasman-uni.example.
