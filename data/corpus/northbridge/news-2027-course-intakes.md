# Course intakes for 2027 now open

Applications are now open for our 2027 course intakes. Three courses are already in high demand:

- Data Quality Management starts on March 22, 2027
- Data Visualisation and Storytelling starts on 8 March 2027

Early enrolment is recommended, as places are limited and offered in order of application.
