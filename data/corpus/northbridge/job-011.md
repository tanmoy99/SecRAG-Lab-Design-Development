# Job: Data Steward at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Data Steward to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; coach business teams in using data in their decisions.

Salary: AUD 105,000 to AUD 120,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
