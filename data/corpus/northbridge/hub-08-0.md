# Ethical review of analytics: common mistakes to avoid

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. An ethics checklist is a starting point for discussion, not a box-ticking exercise.

Documenting the reasons for a decision protects both the organisation and the people affected. Community expectations are a useful test: would the people in the data be surprised by this use?

Linking data sets can reveal far more about people than either data set does alone. Not every analytics project needs a formal ethics review, but every project should ask who could be harmed.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Data Ethics in Practice, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
