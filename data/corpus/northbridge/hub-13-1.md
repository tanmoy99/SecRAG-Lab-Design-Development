# Incident response for data breaches: a practical introduction

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. Practising the plan with a short tabletop exercise reveals gaps before a real incident does.

The first hours of an incident are about containment and preserving evidence. Assessing whether a breach is likely to cause serious harm determines whether notification is required.

A data breach response plan should name who decides, who communicates and who investigates. After an incident, a blameless review helps the organisation learn rather than hide mistakes.

This topic is covered in more depth in SC310 Security for Data Professionals.
