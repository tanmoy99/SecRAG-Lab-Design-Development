# Data Visualisation and Storytelling (VZ140)

Shows how to design charts and dashboards that help decision makers understand data quickly. It is aimed at working professionals and assumes no prior formal training in the area.

## What you will learn

- Choosing the right chart
- Colour and accessibility
- Dashboard design
- Presenting data to executives

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: A$2,150 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

Classes start on Monday 8 March 2027. The course runs for 10 weeks, with about six hours of study per week. Delivery is on campus at the Harbour campus.

## Course coordinator

VZ140 is coordinated by Yuki Murray. Questions about content or assessment can be sent to yuki.murray@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.

Applications open about eight weeks before each intake. Once your enrolment is confirmed you will receive access to the learning portal. If you need to withdraw, see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
