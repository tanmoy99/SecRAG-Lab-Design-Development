# Applied Analytics with Python (AN230)

A hands-on course in cleaning, analysing and reporting on data with Python and pandas. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Working with pandas data frames
- Exploratory data analysis
- Reproducible notebooks
- Communicating analytical results

## Fees, dates and delivery

The fee for AN230 is AUD 2,795. Members of the Institute receive priority enrolment but pay the same fee.

Duration: 8 weeks. Next start date: 26 July 2027. Delivery is online, with live webinars each week.

## Course coordinator

AN230 is coordinated by Noah Iyer. Questions about content or assessment can be sent to noah.iyer@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.
