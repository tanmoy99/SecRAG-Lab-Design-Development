# Master data management: a checklist for small teams

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. A single golden record is less important than agreeing which system is authoritative for each attribute.

Duplicate customer records are the most common symptom of weak master data management. Master data programs fail when they are run as IT projects without business owners.

Master data is the shared reference data, such as customers, products and locations, that many systems use. Matching rules should be tested on real data before they are switched on in production.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Foundations of Data Governance, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
