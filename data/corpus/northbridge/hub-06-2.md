# Records disposal: lessons from the field

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Disposing of records on schedule reduces storage costs and the impact of any data breach.

Records should only be destroyed under an approved retention and disposal authority. A disposal register shows what was destroyed, when, and under which authority.

Digital records are often kept forever by default because no one is responsible for deleting them. Legal holds override normal disposal schedules when litigation or an investigation is likely.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Records and Information Management, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
