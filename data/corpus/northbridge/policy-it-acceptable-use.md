# IT Acceptable Use Policy

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Institute's IT systems, including staff, students, contractors and volunteers.

## Passwords and sign-in

All accounts must use multi-factor authentication and a password of no fewer than 12 characters.

## Reporting problems

Report lost devices, suspicious emails or suspected security incidents to the IT service desk at servicedesk@northbridge.example as soon as possible.
