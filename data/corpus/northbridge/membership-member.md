# Member membership

Member membership is for practitioners with at least three years of relevant professional experience.

Member members pay $415.00 per year.

To keep their membership current, Member members must complete twenty hours of continuing professional development (CPD) each membership year.

Membership gives you access to the member forum and knowledge hub, member pricing for events, and the right to vote at the Annual General Meeting.

Apply or renew through the member portal. The Membership team reviews applications within a few working days.
