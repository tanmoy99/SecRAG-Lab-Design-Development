# Associate membership

Associate membership is for people starting out in a data role with less than three years of relevant experience.

The annual fee for Associate membership is $185.

Associate members commit to 10 hours of CPD per year and record their activities in the member portal.

Membership gives you access to the member forum and knowledge hub, member pricing for events, and the right to vote at the Annual General Meeting.

Apply or renew through the member portal. The Membership team reviews applications within a few working days.
