# Finance and Audit Committee: minutes of 19 October 2025

Present: Leila Karimi (chair), Callum Rahman, Noah Iyer, Chloe Wiremu.

The committee discussed a proposal to refresh the assessment rubric for Data Ethics in Practice. Further work is needed on how group work is marked.

The committee discussed sponsorship options for the Data Quality Masterclass. Callum Rahman will follow up with two possible sponsors.

An update was given on the student portal upgrade. Testing with a small group of students is under way.

The meeting closed at the scheduled time. The next meeting will be held next quarter.
