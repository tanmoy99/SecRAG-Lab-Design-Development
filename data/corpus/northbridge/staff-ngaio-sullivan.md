# Staff profile: Ngaio Sullivan

As Program Coordinator, Ngaio Sullivan designs and runs courses in the Institute's catalogue, working with industry practitioners who teach as guest lecturers.

Ngaio currently coordinates Foundations of Data Governance (DG101); Security for Data Professionals (SC310); Cloud Data Platforms (CL250).

To get in touch with Ngaio, email ngaio.sullivan@northbridge.example or call the main switchboard and ask for extension 4677.
