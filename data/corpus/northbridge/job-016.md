# Job: Data Governance Analyst at Coastal Health Network

Coastal Health Network is looking for a Data Governance Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects.

Salary: AUD 130,000 to AUD 145,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@coastalhealth.example.
