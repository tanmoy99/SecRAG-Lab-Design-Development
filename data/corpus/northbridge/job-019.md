# Job: Data Engineer at Ironbark Insurance

Ironbark Insurance is looking for a Data Engineer to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; coach business teams in using data in their decisions.

Salary: AUD 115,000 to AUD 130,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@ironbark.example.
