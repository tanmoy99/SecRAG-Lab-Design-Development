# Job: Data Governance Analyst at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Data Governance Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary; support the response to information security incidents; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform.

Salary: AUD 125,000 to AUD 140,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
