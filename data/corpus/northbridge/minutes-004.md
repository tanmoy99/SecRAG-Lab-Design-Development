# Events Working Group: minutes of 30 July 2025

Present: Mei Sato (chair), Yuki Murray, Arjun Hosseini, Chloe Wiremu.

The draft program for Foundations of Data Governance was endorsed, subject to minor edits to the reading list.

The committee agreed to review the format of the member newsletter. Arjun Hosseini will circulate options.

The committee discussed a proposal to refresh the assessment rubric for Data Quality Management. Further work is needed on how group work is marked.

The meeting closed at the scheduled time. The next meeting will be held next quarter.
