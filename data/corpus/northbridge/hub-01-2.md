# Master data management: a practical introduction

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. Duplicate customer records are the most common symptom of weak master data management.

Master data programs fail when they are run as IT projects without business owners. A single golden record is less important than agreeing which system is authoritative for each attribute.

Matching rules should be tested on real data before they are switched on in production. Master data is the shared reference data, such as customers, products and locations, that many systems use.
