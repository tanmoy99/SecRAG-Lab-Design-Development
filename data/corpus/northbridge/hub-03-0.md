# Privacy by design: lessons from the field

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Default settings matter: most users never change them, so the default should be the private option.

Privacy by design means building privacy protections into a system from the start rather than adding them later. Collecting less personal information is the simplest and most effective privacy control.

Retention periods should be decided when data is collected, not when storage runs out. A privacy impact assessment early in a project is far cheaper than redesigning a finished system.

This topic is covered in more depth in PR210 Privacy Law for Practitioners.
