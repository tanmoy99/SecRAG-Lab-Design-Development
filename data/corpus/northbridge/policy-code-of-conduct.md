# Code of Conduct

All members, students and staff are expected to act with integrity, respect and professionalism in their dealings with the Institute and with each other.

Members must not misrepresent their membership tier or qualifications, and must declare conflicts of interest when acting on Institute committees.

Concerns about a possible breach of this Code should be raised with the Registrar, Tomas Silva, who will decide whether a formal investigation is needed.
