# Job: Information Security Analyst at Coastal Health Network

Coastal Health Network is looking for a Information Security Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; coach business teams in using data in their decisions.

Salary: AUD 120,000 to AUD 135,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@coastalhealth.example.
