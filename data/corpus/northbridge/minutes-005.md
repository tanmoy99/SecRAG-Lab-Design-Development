# Education Committee: minutes of 14 September 2025

Present: Samira McKenzie (chair), Callum Rahman, Leila Karimi.

Callum Rahman reported on planning for the Privacy Awareness Week Breakfast. Speaker invitations have been sent and the venue is booked.

The committee discussed a proposal to refresh the assessment rubric for Security for Data Professionals. Further work is needed on how group work is marked.

The committee discussed sponsorship options for the Women in Data Leadership Forum. Callum Rahman will follow up with two possible sponsors.

The meeting closed at the scheduled time. The next meeting will be held next quarter.
