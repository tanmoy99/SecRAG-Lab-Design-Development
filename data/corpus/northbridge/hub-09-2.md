# Model monitoring: lessons from the field

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Simple models that are well monitored are usually safer than complex models that are not.

A model that performed well at launch can degrade silently as the data it sees changes. Monitoring input data distributions often reveals drift before accuracy drops.

Every production model should have an owner and a documented process for retraining or retiring it. Logging predictions and outcomes is the foundation of any model monitoring program.
