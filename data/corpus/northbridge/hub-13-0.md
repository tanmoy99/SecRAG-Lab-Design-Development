# Incident response for data breaches: questions to ask before you start

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. A data breach response plan should name who decides, who communicates and who investigates.

The first hours of an incident are about containment and preserving evidence. Assessing whether a breach is likely to cause serious harm determines whether notification is required.

Practising the plan with a short tabletop exercise reveals gaps before a real incident does. After an incident, a blameless review helps the organisation learn rather than hide mistakes.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Security for Data Professionals, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
