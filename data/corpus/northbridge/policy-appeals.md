# Assessment Appeals Policy

Students may appeal a final grade or an assessment decision if they believe it was not made in line with the published assessment rubric or Institute policy.

## Lodging an appeal

You have fourteen days from the release of a result to lodge an appeal through the student portal.

Appeals are reviewed by an academic who was not involved in the original marking. The outcome is sent to the student in writing.
