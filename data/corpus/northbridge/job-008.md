# Job: Data Governance Analyst at Kookaburra Retail Group

Kookaburra Retail Group is looking for a Data Governance Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders.

Salary: AUD 110,000 to AUD 125,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@kookaburraretail.example.
