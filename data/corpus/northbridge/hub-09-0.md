# Model monitoring: a practical introduction

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. Logging predictions and outcomes is the foundation of any model monitoring program.

Simple models that are well monitored are usually safer than complex models that are not. A model that performed well at launch can degrade silently as the data it sees changes.

Every production model should have an owner and a documented process for retraining or retiring it. Monitoring input data distributions often reveals drift before accuracy drops.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Machine Learning Operations, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
