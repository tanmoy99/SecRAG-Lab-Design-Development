# Dashboard design: a practical introduction

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Every dashboard should say where its data comes from and when it was last refreshed.

A good dashboard answers a small number of questions for a specific audience. Consistent colours and clear labels matter more than visual effects.

Too many charts on one screen make it harder, not easier, to see what matters. Ask users what decisions the dashboard supports before choosing any charts.
