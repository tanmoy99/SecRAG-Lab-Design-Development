# Metadata and Data Catalogues (MD180)

Explains how metadata and a well-run data catalogue help people find and trust data. It is aimed at working professionals and assumes no prior formal training in the area.

## What you will learn

- Business and technical metadata
- Data lineage
- Driving catalogue adoption
- Business glossary management

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: A$2,740 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

Classes start on 05/07/2027. The course runs for ten weeks, with about six hours of study per week. Delivery is blended: online study plus two full-day workshops at the City campus.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.
