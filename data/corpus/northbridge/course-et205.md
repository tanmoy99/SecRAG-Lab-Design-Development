# Data Ethics in Practice (ET205)

Explores the ethical questions raised by collecting, linking and using data about people. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Consent and transparency
- Fairness and bias in data use
- Ethical review processes
- Community expectations and social licence

## Fees, dates and delivery

The fee for ET205 is $1,010.00. Members of the Institute receive priority enrolment but pay the same fee.

The next intake begins on 07/06/2027 and runs for eight weeks. Delivery is online, with live webinars each week.

## Course coordinator

Your course coordinator is Callum Rahman (callum.rahman@northbridge.example), who can help with questions about the content, assessment or study load.

## Assessment

Assessment is based on two written assignments and a final project that applies the course content to a real or realistic workplace problem. There is no closed-book exam.
