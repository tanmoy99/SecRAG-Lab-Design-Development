# Enrolment FAQ

**Can I get a refund if I withdraw?**
Yes, if you withdraw within thirty days of enrolling. An administration fee of $65.00 applies.

**What happens if I pay my invoice late?**
A late fee of $55.00 is charged once the grace period has passed.

**When does Foundations of Data Governance next start?**
The next intake of DG101 starts on Monday 19 April 2027.

**Can my employer pay?**
Yes. Ask for an invoice during enrolment and we will send it to your employer.
