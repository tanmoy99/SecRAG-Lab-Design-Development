# Privacy by design: common mistakes to avoid

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Collecting less personal information is the simplest and most effective privacy control.

Privacy by design means building privacy protections into a system from the start rather than adding them later. Default settings matter: most users never change them, so the default should be the private option.

A privacy impact assessment early in a project is far cheaper than redesigning a finished system. Retention periods should be decided when data is collected, not when storage runs out.
