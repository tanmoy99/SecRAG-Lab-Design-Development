# Business glossaries: lessons from the field

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Each glossary term needs an owner who can approve changes to its definition.

A business glossary defines the terms an organisation uses, such as 'active member' or 'enrolment'. A glossary that is not maintained quickly loses trust and stops being used.

Arguments about numbers in meetings are often really arguments about definitions. Link glossary terms to the reports and data sets that use them so people can find the definition in context.
