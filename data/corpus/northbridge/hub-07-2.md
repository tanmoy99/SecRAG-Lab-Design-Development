# Access control: lessons from the field

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Least privilege means each person can reach only the data they need for their role.

Removing access promptly when staff change roles is as important as granting it correctly. Access reviews are often skipped because managers find them tedious and unclear.

Small organisations can get most of the benefit of access control with a few well-designed groups. Shared accounts make it impossible to tell who did what, so they should be phased out.
