# Leadership in Data Strategy (LD400)

For senior practitioners who want to set and lead an organisation-wide data strategy. It is aimed at working professionals and assumes no prior formal training in the area.

## What you will learn

- Aligning data strategy with business goals
- Building and growing a data team
- Writing investment cases
- Leading organisational change

## Fees, dates and delivery

The fee for LD400 is $975. Members of the Institute receive priority enrolment but pay the same fee.

Classes start on 8 February 2027. The course runs for 16 weeks, with about six hours of study per week. Delivery is on campus at the Harbour campus.

## Course coordinator

Your course coordinator is Callum Rahman (callum.rahman@northbridge.example), who can help with questions about the content, assessment or study load.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.

## How to enrol

Applications open about eight weeks before each intake. Once your enrolment is confirmed you will receive access to the learning portal. If you need to withdraw, see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
