# Data literacy: what we learned this year

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Data literacy is the ability to read, question and communicate with data in everyday work.

Measuring data literacy with a short self-assessment gives a baseline to improve against. Most organisations overestimate how comfortable their staff are with basic charts and percentages.

Managers set the tone: if they ask where a number came from, their teams start asking too. Short, practical sessions built around staff's own reports work better than generic training.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Data Visualisation and Storytelling, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
