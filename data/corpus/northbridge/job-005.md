# Job: Information Security Analyst at Kookaburra Retail Group

Kookaburra Retail Group is looking for a Information Security Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects.

Salary: AUD 95,000 to AUD 110,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@kookaburraretail.example.
