# Master data management: common mistakes to avoid

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. A single golden record is less important than agreeing which system is authoritative for each attribute.

Matching rules should be tested on real data before they are switched on in production. Master data is the shared reference data, such as customers, products and locations, that many systems use.

Duplicate customer records are the most common symptom of weak master data management. Master data programs fail when they are run as IT projects without business owners.
