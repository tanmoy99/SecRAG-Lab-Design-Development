# Education Committee: minutes of 16 March 2026

Present: Samira McKenzie (chair), Leila Karimi, Ravi Raman, Isla Nguyen.

The committee discussed sponsorship options for the Women in Data Leadership Forum. Isla Nguyen will follow up with two possible sponsors.

The committee discussed a proposal to refresh the assessment rubric for Foundations of Data Governance. Further work is needed on how group work is marked.

The draft program for Data Visualisation and Storytelling was endorsed, subject to minor edits to the reading list.

The meeting closed at the scheduled time. The next meeting will be held next quarter.
