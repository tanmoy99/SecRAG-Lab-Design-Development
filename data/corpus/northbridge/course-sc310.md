# Security for Data Professionals (SC310)

An introduction to information security controls for people who manage or analyse data. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Threat modelling
- Access control and least privilege
- Encryption basics
- Incident response

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: $1,455 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

The next intake begins on August 16, 2027 and runs for six weeks. Delivery is on campus at the Harbour campus.

## Course coordinator

SC310 is coordinated by Ngaio Sullivan. Questions about content or assessment can be sent to ngaio.sullivan@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Assessment is based on two written assignments and a final project that applies the course content to a real or realistic workplace problem. There is no closed-book exam.

## How to enrol

Applications open about eight weeks before each intake. Once your enrolment is confirmed you will receive access to the learning portal. If you need to withdraw, see the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
