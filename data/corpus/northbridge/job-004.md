# Job: Data Steward at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Data Steward to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects.

Salary: AUD 135,000 to AUD 150,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
