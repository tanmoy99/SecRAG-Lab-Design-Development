# Job: Senior Data Analyst at Wattle Council

Wattle Council is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will manage records retention and disposal across the organisation; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects; design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform.

Salary: AUD 80,000 to AUD 95,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@wattlecouncil.example.
