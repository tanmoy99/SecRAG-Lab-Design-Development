# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains when course fees are refunded if a participant withdraws from a course, and what happens if the Institute cancels a course.

## Refund window

A full refund, minus a AUD 65 administration fee, is available if you withdraw no later than 30 days after your enrolment is confirmed.

Withdrawals after the refund window has closed are not refunded, except in documented cases of serious illness, bereavement or other exceptional circumstances, which the Registrar assesses case by case.

## Course cancellations

If the Institute cancels a course, all fees are refunded in full and no administration fee is charged. Participants may instead transfer to a later intake.

## How to apply

Refund requests are made through the student portal. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method.
