# Job: Business Intelligence Developer at Coastal Health Network

Coastal Health Network is looking for a Business Intelligence Developer to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation.

Salary: AUD 115,000 to AUD 130,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@coastalhealth.example.
