# Ethical review of analytics: what we learned this year

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Community expectations are a useful test: would the people in the data be surprised by this use?

Documenting the reasons for a decision protects both the organisation and the people affected. Linking data sets can reveal far more about people than either data set does alone.

Not every analytics project needs a formal ethics review, but every project should ask who could be harmed. An ethics checklist is a starting point for discussion, not a box-ticking exercise.
