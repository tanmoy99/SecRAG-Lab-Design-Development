# Job: Data Engineer at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Data Engineer to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; support the response to information security incidents; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation.

Salary: AUD 115,000 to AUD 130,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
