# Privacy Policy

The Institute collects personal information about students, members, staff and event attendees to provide its services. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

## Contacting us about privacy

Our Privacy Officer is Ravi Raman. You can reach the privacy team at privacy@northbridge.example.

## Data breaches

If a data breach is likely to result in serious harm, we will notify affected individuals and the regulator as required by law.
