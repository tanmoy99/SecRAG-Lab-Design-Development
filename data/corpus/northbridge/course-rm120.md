# Records and Information Management (RM120)

Covers the full life cycle of records, from creation and classification to retention and disposal. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Records classification schemes
- Retention and disposal authorities
- Digital recordkeeping systems
- Legal holds and discovery

## Fees, dates and delivery

Enrolling in Records and Information Management costs AUD 590, which covers all learning materials and assessment.

The next intake begins on July 12, 2027 and runs for 12 weeks. Delivery is online, with live webinars each week.

## Course coordinator

RM120 is coordinated by Arjun Hosseini. Questions about content or assessment can be sent to arjun.hosseini@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.
