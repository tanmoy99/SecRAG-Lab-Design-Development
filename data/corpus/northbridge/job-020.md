# Job: Information Security Analyst at Kookaburra Retail Group

Kookaburra Retail Group is looking for a Information Security Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; coach business teams in using data in their decisions; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects.

Salary: AUD 135,000 to AUD 150,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@kookaburraretail.example.
