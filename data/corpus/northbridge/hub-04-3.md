# Data quality scorecards: a practical introduction

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. Publish the scorecard where the data's users will see it, not only in a governance committee pack.

Trend lines are more useful than single scores, because they show whether quality is improving. A data quality scorecard reports a handful of measures, such as completeness and validity, for key data sets.

Avoid measuring everything; a few measures tied to business outcomes get attention. Scorecards only change behaviour when each measure has a named owner who can act on it.

This topic is covered in more depth in DQ150 Data Quality Management.
