# Job: Senior Data Analyst at Kookaburra Retail Group

Kookaburra Retail Group is looking for a Senior Data Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will design and maintain data pipelines on a cloud platform; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects.

Salary: AUD 90,000 to AUD 105,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@kookaburraretail.example.
