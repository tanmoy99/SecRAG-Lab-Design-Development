# Membership fees for 2027

The following annual membership fees apply to memberships starting or renewing from 1 January 2027. Fees are GST-free and cover a twelve-month membership period.

| Membership tier | Annual fee |
|---|---|
| Student | A$90 |
| Associate | A$185 |
| Member | $415.00 |
| Fellow | $635 |

Members who join part-way through the year pay a pro-rata fee for the remainder of the membership year. Upgrades between tiers are charged at the difference between the two fees.

Payment can be made by card through the member portal or by bank transfer on an invoice. Members who are experiencing financial hardship can ask the Membership team about a payment plan.
