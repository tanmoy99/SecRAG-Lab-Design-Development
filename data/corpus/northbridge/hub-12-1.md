# Data strategy: a practical introduction

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. The best data strategies are short enough for the executive team to remember.

A data strategy should explain how data will help the organisation achieve its goals, not list technologies. Data strategies fail most often for lack of sustained executive sponsorship.

Quick wins in the first months build support for the longer-term parts of a strategy. Review the strategy each year against what the organisation has actually delivered.
