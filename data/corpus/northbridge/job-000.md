# Job: Data Engineer at Southern Water Authority

Southern Water Authority is looking for a Data Engineer to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will coach business teams in using data in their decisions; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects; maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary.

Salary: AUD 95,000 to AUD 110,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@southernwater.example.
