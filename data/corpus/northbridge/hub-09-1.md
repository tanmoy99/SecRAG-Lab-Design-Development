# Model monitoring: common mistakes to avoid

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. A model that performed well at launch can degrade silently as the data it sees changes.

Monitoring input data distributions often reveals drift before accuracy drops. Logging predictions and outcomes is the foundation of any model monitoring program.

Every production model should have an owner and a documented process for retraining or retiring it. Simple models that are well monitored are usually safer than complex models that are not.
