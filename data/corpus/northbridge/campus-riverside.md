# Riverside campus

The Riverside campus is located at 12 Paperbark Road, South Brisbane QLD 4101. It hosts classes, workshops and member events in Queensland.

## Contact and opening hours

Reception can be reached on +61 7 5550 5307. The campus is open from 9.00am to 5:00 pm, Monday to Friday.

## Getting here

Public transport is the easiest way to reach the campus. There is bicycle parking in the building and a commercial car park within two blocks.

## Accessibility

All teaching rooms are step-free and have hearing loops. Please contact Student Services before your visit if you have specific access requirements.
