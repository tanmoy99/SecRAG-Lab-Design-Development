# Business glossaries: what we learned this year

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. A business glossary defines the terms an organisation uses, such as 'active member' or 'enrolment'.

Arguments about numbers in meetings are often really arguments about definitions. Link glossary terms to the reports and data sets that use them so people can find the definition in context.

Each glossary term needs an owner who can approve changes to its definition. A glossary that is not maintained quickly loses trust and stops being used.

This topic is covered in more depth in MD180 Metadata and Data Catalogues.
