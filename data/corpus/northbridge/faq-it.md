# IT and student portal FAQ

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Use the reset link on the sign-in page, or email servicedesk@northbridge.example.

**What are the password rules?**
Passwords need at least 12 characters, and multi-factor authentication must be switched on.

**Can I use my own laptop?**
Yes. The student portal works in any modern web browser.
