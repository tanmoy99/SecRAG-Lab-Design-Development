# Data strategy: lessons from the field

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. The best data strategies are short enough for the executive team to remember.

Quick wins in the first months build support for the longer-term parts of a strategy. Data strategies fail most often for lack of sustained executive sponsorship.

A data strategy should explain how data will help the organisation achieve its goals, not list technologies. Review the strategy each year against what the organisation has actually delivered.
