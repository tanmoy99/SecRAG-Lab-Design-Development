# Fees and Late Payment Policy

This policy applies to course fees, membership fees and event invoices issued by the Institute.

## Late payment

A grace period of 10 days applies after the due date. After that, a late fee of A$55 is charged.

## Payment plans

Students and members experiencing financial hardship can apply for a payment plan before the due date. No late fee is charged while an approved payment plan is being followed.
