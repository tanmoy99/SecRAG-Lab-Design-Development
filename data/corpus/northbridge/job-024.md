# Job: Information Security Analyst at Southern Water Authority

Southern Water Authority is looking for a Information Security Analyst to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will support the response to information security incidents; prepare privacy impact assessments for new projects; run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects.

Salary: AUD 110,000 to AUD 125,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@southernwater.example.
