# Foundations of Data Governance (DG101)

This course introduces the roles, policies and decision rights that let an organisation treat data as a managed asset. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Data ownership and stewardship roles
- Writing a data governance charter
- Decision rights and escalation paths
- Measuring governance maturity

## Fees, dates and delivery

The fee for DG101 is $1,355. Members of the Institute receive priority enrolment but pay the same fee.

The next intake begins on April 19, 2027 and runs for ten weeks. Delivery is blended: online study plus two full-day workshops at the City campus.

## Course coordinator

## Assessment

Assessment is based on two written assignments and a final project that applies the course content to a real or realistic workplace problem. There is no closed-book exam.
