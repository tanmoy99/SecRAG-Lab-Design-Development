# Data quality scorecards: lessons from the field

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Scorecards only change behaviour when each measure has a named owner who can act on it.

Avoid measuring everything; a few measures tied to business outcomes get attention. A data quality scorecard reports a handful of measures, such as completeness and validity, for key data sets.

Publish the scorecard where the data's users will see it, not only in a governance committee pack. Trend lines are more useful than single scores, because they show whether quality is improving.
