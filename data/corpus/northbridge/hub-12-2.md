# Data strategy: questions to ask before you start

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. Data strategies fail most often for lack of sustained executive sponsorship.

The best data strategies are short enough for the executive team to remember. Review the strategy each year against what the organisation has actually delivered.

Quick wins in the first months build support for the longer-term parts of a strategy. A data strategy should explain how data will help the organisation achieve its goals, not list technologies.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Leadership in Data Strategy, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
