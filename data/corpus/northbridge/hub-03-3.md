# Privacy by design: a practical introduction

This short piece draws on discussions at recent member events. Retention periods should be decided when data is collected, not when storage runs out.

A privacy impact assessment early in a project is far cheaper than redesigning a finished system. Privacy by design means building privacy protections into a system from the start rather than adding them later.

Default settings matter: most users never change them, so the default should be the private option. Collecting less personal information is the simplest and most effective privacy control.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Privacy Law for Practitioners, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
