# Cloud Data Platforms (CL250)

An overview of storing and processing organisational data on modern cloud platforms. The course suits people who already work with data and want a structured grounding in the topic.

## What you will learn

- Data lakes and warehouses
- Cloud cost management
- The shared responsibility model for security
- Migration planning

## Fees, dates and delivery

The fee for CL250 is AUD 770. Members of the Institute receive priority enrolment but pay the same fee.

The next intake begins on Monday 5 April 2027 and runs for 16 weeks. Delivery is blended: online study plus two full-day workshops at the Harbour campus.

## Course coordinator

CL250 is coordinated by Ngaio Sullivan. Questions about content or assessment can be sent to ngaio.sullivan@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.
