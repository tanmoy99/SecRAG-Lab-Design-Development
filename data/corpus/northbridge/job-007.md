# Job: Records Manager at Meridian Logistics

Meridian Logistics is looking for a Records Manager to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will run data quality checks and work with system owners to fix defects; maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary; manage records retention and disposal across the organisation.

Salary: AUD 120,000 to AUD 135,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@meridianlogistics.example.
