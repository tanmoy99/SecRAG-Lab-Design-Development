# Records Retention Schedule

This schedule sets out how long the Institute keeps different kinds of records before they are securely destroyed or archived.

## Student records

Student enrolment and assessment records are kept for 8 years after the student's final enrolment.

## Other records

Financial records are kept as required by taxation law. General correspondence is kept for as long as it is needed for business purposes and then destroyed.
