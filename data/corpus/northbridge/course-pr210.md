# Privacy Law for Practitioners (PR210)

A practical course on the Australian Privacy Principles and how they apply to everyday data handling. Most participants study part-time alongside their job.

## What you will learn

- The Australian Privacy Principles
- Notifiable data breaches
- Privacy impact assessments
- Cross-border disclosure of personal information

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: A$2,035 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

Classes start on Monday 3 May 2027. The course runs for ten weeks, with about six hours of study per week. Delivery is on campus at the Riverside campus.

## Course coordinator

Your course coordinator is Callum Rahman (callum.rahman@northbridge.example), who can help with questions about the content, assessment or study load.

## Assessment

Participants complete weekly online quizzes, one case-study report and a short recorded presentation. Each piece of assessment is marked against a published rubric.

## How to enrol

To enrol, create an account on the student portal and select the intake you want. Employers can pay on behalf of staff by requesting an invoice. Refunds are governed by the Refund and Cancellation Policy.
