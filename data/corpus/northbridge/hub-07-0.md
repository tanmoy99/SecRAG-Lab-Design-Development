# Access control: a practical introduction

Members often ask us where to start with this topic, so we have collected some practical advice. Least privilege means each person can reach only the data they need for their role.

Small organisations can get most of the benefit of access control with a few well-designed groups. Removing access promptly when staff change roles is as important as granting it correctly.

Access reviews are often skipped because managers find them tedious and unclear. Shared accounts make it impossible to tell who did what, so they should be phased out.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Security for Data Professionals, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
