# Incident response for data breaches: what we learned this year

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. The first hours of an incident are about containment and preserving evidence.

After an incident, a blameless review helps the organisation learn rather than hide mistakes. Practising the plan with a short tabletop exercise reveals gaps before a real incident does.

A data breach response plan should name who decides, who communicates and who investigates. Assessing whether a breach is likely to cause serious harm determines whether notification is required.

This topic is covered in more depth in SC310 Security for Data Professionals.
