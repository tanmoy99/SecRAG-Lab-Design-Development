# Machine Learning Operations (ML320)

Covers the practices needed to deploy, monitor and maintain machine learning models in production. It is aimed at working professionals and assumes no prior formal training in the area.

## What you will learn

- Model versioning
- Monitoring for data and model drift
- Deployment pipelines
- Model risk management

## Fees, dates and delivery

Course fee: A$2,415 (GST-free). Fees are payable in full at enrolment.

The next intake begins on Monday 17 May 2027 and runs for ten weeks. Delivery is on campus at the Harbour campus.

## Course coordinator

For questions about this course, contact the coordinator, Noah Iyer, at noah.iyer@northbridge.example.

## Assessment

Assessment is based on two written assignments and a final project that applies the course content to a real or realistic workplace problem. There is no closed-book exam.
