# IT Steering Group: minutes of 6 July 2025

Present: Mateo Haddad (chair), Mei Sato, Leila Karimi.

The committee reviewed learner feedback for Applied Analytics with Python. Participants asked for more worked examples; Mei Sato will draft additional case material before the next intake.

The committee discussed a proposal to refresh the assessment rubric for Applied Analytics with Python. Further work is needed on how group work is marked.

An update was given on the student portal upgrade. Testing with a small group of students is under way.

The meeting closed at the scheduled time. The next meeting will be held next quarter.
