# Master data management: what we learned this year

This article is part of the knowledge hub series for members working in small and medium organisations. A single golden record is less important than agreeing which system is authoritative for each attribute.

Master data programs fail when they are run as IT projects without business owners. Master data is the shared reference data, such as customers, products and locations, that many systems use.

Matching rules should be tested on real data before they are switched on in production. Duplicate customer records are the most common symptom of weak master data management.

This topic is covered in more depth in DG101 Foundations of Data Governance.
