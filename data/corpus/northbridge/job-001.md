# Job: Records Manager at Bluegum Analytics

Bluegum Analytics is looking for a Records Manager to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary; build and maintain dashboards for senior leaders; support the response to information security incidents.

Salary: AUD 80,000 to AUD 95,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@bluegum.example.
