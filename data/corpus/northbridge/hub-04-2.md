# Data quality scorecards: what we learned this year

The following notes summarise a talk given at a member networking evening. Trend lines are more useful than single scores, because they show whether quality is improving.

Scorecards only change behaviour when each measure has a named owner who can act on it. A data quality scorecard reports a handful of measures, such as completeness and validity, for key data sets.

Publish the scorecard where the data's users will see it, not only in a governance committee pack. Avoid measuring everything; a few measures tied to business outcomes get attention.

Members who want to go further may be interested in Data Quality Management, listed in the Institute's course catalogue.
