# Fellow membership

Fellow membership is awarded by invitation to senior practitioners who have made a significant contribution to the profession.

The annual fee for Fellow membership is $635.00.

Fellow members commit to twenty-five hours of CPD per year and record their activities in the member portal.

Benefits include discounted event tickets, access to the members-only knowledge hub, the monthly newsletter and the member forum.

Apply or renew through the member portal. The Membership team reviews applications within a few working days.
