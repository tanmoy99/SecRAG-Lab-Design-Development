# Job: Data Steward at Tasman University

Tasman University is looking for a Data Steward to join a small, friendly team.

In this role you will maintain the organisation's data catalogue and business glossary; support the response to information security incidents; coach business teams in using data in their decisions.

Salary: AUD 120,000 to AUD 135,000 plus superannuation. Hybrid work is available.

Membership of a professional body is an advantage. Apply by email to careers@tasman-uni.example.
